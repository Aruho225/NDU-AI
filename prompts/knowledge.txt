## University overview
- Motto: Fear of God brings knowledge and Wisdom.
- Founded: 1992. Championing private, chartered Christian-based education in Uganda.
- Mission: Provide Christian-based high quality and innovative teaching, research and outreach services.
- Vision: Be the leading Christian-based University for societal transformation.
- Ndejje University is a pioneered private university in Uganda, chartered and licensed by the Ministry of Education and Sports.
- The university offers over 120 academic programmes across 7 faculties and maintains high ethical standards.
- Official website: https://ndejjeuniversity.ac.ug/
- Website tagline: Excellence in Education, integrity in Service.
- Owned by the six Church of Uganda Dioceses in Buganda Region (Ndejje University Foundation Consortium).
- Chartered by NCHE; about 10,000 students and 500 staff in Uganda.

## Campus locations
- Main Campus: Ndejje Hill, 8 km (5 miles) north-west of Bombo Town, 32 km (20 miles) on the Kampala-Gulu Road. Includes Lady Irene Campus.
- Kampala Campus: Mengo, Plot 151 Balintuma Road.
- Graduate School: Muteesa I Road, Rubaga Hill, near Rubaga Hospital and Rubaga Cathedral.

## Entry requirements
- Postgraduate: At least a Second Class Honours degree from a recognized institution. Exceptional cases may require professional qualifications and at least two years of relevant work experience.
- Undergraduate: Minimum of two relevant Principal Passes in UACE obtained at the same sitting, or equivalent.
- Diploma: Minimum one Principal Pass in UACE, or a Certificate with at least Credit or 2nd Class, plus at least five passes at O Level (UCE).

## Application process
1. Pay the application fee.
2. Collect, fill, and return application forms to the Academic Registrar's Office at either campus.
3. Submit copies of O Level (UCE) and A Level (UACE) slips or certificates.
4. Submit one coloured passport-size photograph.
- Online applications: https://admissions.ndu.ac.ug
- Application fees: Undergraduate UGX 50,000 ($14); Postgraduate UGX 70,000 ($20).
- Intake periods: January and August.
- Study sessions: Day, Weekend, In-Service, and Online.
